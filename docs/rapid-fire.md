# PR7 — Rapid-fire celebrations and scoring

A verified first arrival triggers the celebration automatically. The exact AttemptResult from the successful launch is retained; each remaining Pip follows its pointAt function. No equation re-reading, new collision decision or manual launch is required. The waiting queue gets an excited pause, then Pips leave at staggered intervals and overlap in flight. Recoil, small gold trails and portal sparkles accompany each arrival. Reduced motion uses discrete positions, no trail/recoil/tilt, shorter timing and static sparkles.

The state machine has a celebrating phase with a unique ID. A monotone timeline tracks dispatch (when a creature leaves the queue to load) and arrival (at the exact exit), not just the start of a celebration. Waiting + in flight + failed + arrived = total throughout. The terminal card and star rating appear only after all remaining Pips arrive and the visual tail ends. A success with no waiting Pips skips rapid-fire. Editor, Launch and Restart stay locked until the celebration ends; camera inspection stays available. Failed attempts remain failed. Scores: five saved = three stars, three/four = two, one/two = one, zero = zero. For other budgets use all saved for three and at least 60% for two. No persistent star storage until the planned progress PR.

Existing collision and misfire behavior unchanged. Phase 2 starts with multi-cannon routing, then all-cannon planning, obstacles, feedback, World 1 and World 2.
