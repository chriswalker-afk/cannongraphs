# PR56 — Official Salvage Yard

World 8 is now part of the canonical catalogue: 8 worlds, 96 levels and 288 mode/level combinations. The normal category still contains the original 6 worlds (72 levels); Broken cannons contains Broken Foundry and The Salvage Yard (24 levels). Stable IDs and original level definitions are unchanged. The experimental flight tests remain unranked, outside the catalogue. Official progression follows the Foundry finale through all 12 Salvage puzzles and stops after the finale, with no wraparound.

The world card uses original vector artwork: scrapyard cranes, spare parts, a broken cannon, neutral candidate sockets and a decorative placement arrow. The four tier bands retain their existing colours but describe the anchor mechanism rather than fixed cannon count: Single placements, Connected lines, Locked arcs, Three-part relays. Selected-level details include all inventory graph families, total cannons, candidate mounts, movable/locked part counts and the first two supplied incoming equations. All modes use the same placement puzzle and all levels are unlocked. World music is the original synthesized Salvage shuffle; respects the existing audio settings.

Progress uses the canonical authored level and validated runtime mounts. Cadet completion and Pilot/Ace stars are saved independently, without storing solved mounts or expressions. Existing records and earned badges remain valid. Salvage engineer is earned for completing all 12 levels across any modes. Master explorer now targets all 96 levels; already-retained Master explorer achievements are not revoked. Relay runner includes inventory parts. Existing cosmetics are unchanged (new world achievements are badges only). No new gem locations were added, to preserve the audited puzzles and avoid accidental solution hints.

The reference fixtures and difficulty/support audit now cover all 96 levels, including actual solved anchor layouts, 75 candidate mounts, true flight order (including the nonalphabetical relay), and every mode. Existing exhaustive uniqueness audits remain intact. The official selection and record tests exercise category switching, all 12 tiles, tier labels, completion, reload, mode retention, and the transition from Foundry to Salvage. Browser screenshot attachments enable a visual review at desktop and phone sizes. This is a structural/math audit; classroom difficulty and physical-device experience need human validation.
