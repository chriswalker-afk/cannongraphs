# PR24 — Flight log, progress and achievements

Progress is saved locally on the current browser/origin under cannon-graphs:progress:v1. There is no account, student identifier, network sync or tracking service. Each catalog level has independent Cadet, Pilot and Ace records. A record exists only after the terminal success state (all successful celebration arrivals resolved). Failed flights, provisional gems, incomplete celebrations, home demos, previews and restarts cannot grant progress. No change to unlock/access rules, collision engine, equations or scoring.

Records retain the fewest test flights and the union of unique gem IDs earned on successful runs in that mode. Ranked stars are derived from the fewest attempts and the mode's actual finite budget; Cadet has no ranked stars. Stars and cumulative gems are separate best records, not necessarily achieved in the same flight. Totals across modes count distinct completed levels and distinct (level ID, gem ID) pairs once; mode-specific gem totals count only the selected mode. A replay cannot remove a best record or multiply a gem reward.

The home page links to a Flight log with overall distinct levels, Pilot and Ace stars separately, unique gems, per-mode completion and each world's completion. World cards display distinct completed levels; mode cards and level numbers show mode-specific progress, with completion markers/actual best stars only after a successful run. The selected-level detail shows best attempts, stars (ranked modes) and cumulative gems. The completion panel identifies improved records and newly earned achievements, and distinguishes unsaved session progress when storage fails.

Fifteen achievements have stable IDs and are derived from actual records, not independently editable counters. They cover first completion, mode wings, perfect ranked runs, stars, unique gems, complete gem sets, a multi-cannon level, a full world in one mode, Ace world completion and all 72 levels. This supplies an earnedAchievementIds contract for creature/cannon cosmetics in PR25/26. Achievements are not level locks. No cosmetic selection/unlock UI is implemented yet.

Storage decoding tolerates missing, malformed and unsupported-version data, filters unknown levels/modes/gems and invalid attempt counts, and uses only catalog keys. Missing or blocked storage falls back to in-memory progress and a visible warning. Existing disk records are merged before updates, preserving better records and gems when tabs are used sequentially; simultaneous writes are not an atomic cross-tab transaction. Clearing site data or switching browsers/devices loses access to local records. No arbitrary code is evaluated, and no claim of tamper-proof client-side scoring is made.

Automated tests exercise complete-only recording, mode isolation, better/worse replays, unique gems across modes, achievement thresholds, full-world/all-level predicates, validation, storage roundtrips, quota failures and sequential tab merging. Browser QA remains outstanding: verify actual reload persistence, progress indicators and completion panel on desktop/phone, storage-disabled fallback and navigation.
