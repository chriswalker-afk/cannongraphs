# PR10 — Terrain and obstacles

All solid terrain uses world-aligned axis-aligned rectangles. Authoring helpers in src/levels/terrain.ts create floors, walls, platforms, ceilings and a narrow passage as two solid rectangles separated by empty space. The passage builder rejects a gap at or below the nominal 0.40-world-unit Pip diameter. The continuous swept disk from PR5 remains the source of truth; touching a surface counts as a hit, with solid contact taking precedence over capture. No invisible decorative hitboxes or frame-rate-dependent sampling.

Rendering has a reusable terrain module with clipped original vector masonry, earthy floors, grass-topped walls and platforms, and a darker ceiling with an underside cap. The visible outer rectangle is the collision boundary. Hover/tap/keyboard inspection shows authored x/y extents and dimensions in the toolbar and a multiline tooltip, even when a large obstacle is partly offscreen. Background art is still decorative.

Three selectable meadow previews demonstrate wall clearance, a low ceiling with platform, and a two-cannon relay through a narrow passage. These are development examples, not the final World 1 progression. The original two previews remain available. All five have reference solutions in automated tests only. The authoring QA helper checks anchor clearance, route success, expected cannon sequence and exact minimum centreline clearance minus the nominal Pip radius; it is not imported by the gameplay UI. Deliberately blocked test fixtures remain possible. No prelaunch hints or correct equations are shown.
