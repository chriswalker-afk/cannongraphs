# PR25 — Creature Workshop

A separate workshop opens from Home. Four categories (fur, goggles, headgear and accessories) provide 24 original vector cosmetic choices, eight immediately available and sixteen achievement-gated. Every one of the fifteen existing achievement IDs unlocks at least one item. No currency, random rewards, purchases, new level gates or gameplay advantages. The original varied-colour crew with aviator cap and sky goggles is the default. A selected custom fur is uniform across the crew.

The workshop displays the currently equipped outfit and a live preview of the draft, with idle, happy, flying and disappointed expressions. Each part is independently selectable; locked parts can be tried on in the preview, but Equip is disabled until all draft parts are earned. Lock cards display the exact existing achievement condition. Restore original resets the draft only; leaving the workshop discards unequipped changes. Equip writes the complete look. The Flight log lists cosmetic rewards under their achievements.

The outfit is saved to cannon-graphs:pip-look:v1 separately from progress. Unknown, malformed or no-longer-earned parts fall back to the original part in that slot; unearned outfits cannot be equipped. Storage denial preserves the outfit in session memory with a visible warning. The selected outfit is captured when entering a level, and reused by the waiting/saved/lost queue, the first test flight (including misfire/splat), every rapid-fire Pip, resize/zoom redraws and the home demonstration. Static world and mode postcards retain their original artwork. No changes to the mathematical radius, graph, collision, capture, creature budget, gems, scoring or attempt rules. Hats and pins are decoration only.

Tests check the complete catalog, achievement references and actual unlocks, locked preview/equip separation, default/fallback behaviour, storage roundtrip and failures, and pure appearance colour selection. Browser QA outstanding: workshop card layout and visual clipping on narrow screens, matching preview and in-game art, reverse/misfire/splat/celebration poses, keyboard navigation and real reload.
