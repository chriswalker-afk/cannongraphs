# PR43 — Movable cannon foundations

A lift is optional metadata on a normal linear or quadratic cannon: `lift: { minY, maxY, lockedShape }`. The authored position is its initial muzzle coordinate; runtime copies hold the current position. The rail permits integer y positions, clamps and snaps requested heights, and never changes x, direction, or family. The zero-constant shape is expressed in global x (e.g. `-x` or `-x^2/2+6x`), parsed by the existing bounded polynomial parser. The constant is derived exactly as y minus the nonconstant polynomial evaluated at x. This preserves all nonconstant coefficients and the derivative at every x. Horizontal lines are valid. Fractional x coordinates and rational coefficients are supported; fractional lift stops and advanced families are deferred.

Validation rejects malformed rails, unsupported shapes, out-of-bounds heights and current anchor conflicts with exit, cannon, gems or solid terrain. A rejected placement leaves the previous immutable geometry intact. Rails and moving decks are decorative, not additional collision bodies. A rail may span an obstructed position; the destination is rejected when a player tries to place there.

`createLevelFlightPlan` seeds locked ready equations; normal entries still require programming. `editPlan` refuses locked edits. `moveLiftInPlanning` returns a new level and matching plan only during planning/retry. Movement invalidates every leg's success/failure evidence because capture geometry changed, without deleting ordinary equation drafts. The route engine independently derives the locked equation from the supplied level, rejecting a conflicting supplied equation, and uses real capture, terrain and boundary logic. The attempt snapshot must use the same geometry and plan through animation and celebration.

PR43 is engine/model infrastructure only: no new levels are exposed, the existing 72 levels and all current browser interactions remain unchanged, and the custom level codec still supports the original format. PR44 must wire the level-aware plan into the run, render lifts and drag handles, use camera coordinates and keyboard controls, update scene geometry/preview/feedback, preserve positions on replay and restore authored positions on restart, and freeze movement during flight. Custom level serialization and editor integration are planned for PR47. A failed placement should not consume a Pip. Cadet-only preview remains opt-in.

Tests cover snapping, bounds, exact intercepts, quadratic translations, actual route success/miss and wall clearance, locked edits, invalid destinations, evidence invalidation, and movement restrictions. CI also runs the existing curriculum and browser suites.
