# PR26 — Cannon Workshop

The home page opens a separate Cannon Workshop with six options in each of four categories: barrel shape, mount, trim and firing effect. Eight are free from the start and sixteen require existing achievements. Each of the fifteen achievements supplies at least one cannon reward. The same equipped style is used by all seven families; their original family colours and symbols are fixed, including counter-mirrored lettering on leftward cannons. Original barrels, wooden mounts, matching trim and smoke remain the default.

The draft preview uses the actual cannon SVG renderer and pooled firing particles. Choose any function family, flip the firing direction, inspect a locked choice or test-fire a short recoil/burst. Test fire is a decorative one-shot, not a real attempt or progress event; reduced motion shows a still burst. Leaving the workshop cancels the preview frame. Only a fully unlocked draft can be equipped, and restore original changes the draft until explicitly equipped. The Flight Log lists both Pip and cannon rewards, and completion announcements count new parts from both catalogs.

Styles are saved independently under cannon-graphs:cannon-look:v1. Unknown, invalid or no-longer-earned parts fall back independently to their default. Blocked storage keeps a session-only style with a warning. The equipped style is captured on entering a run and used by the home demonstration, game scene, zoom/resize redraws, single test flights and rapid-fire celebrations. Static selection postcards retain the original art. Burst effects reuse the real launch cue and are hidden when the cue is null (including misfire and reduced motion); gold flight trails and portal effects retain their original meanings.

No changes to mathematical muzzle coordinates, hitboxes, graph simulation, budgets, scoring, gem contacts or level access. Cosmetic mount and barrel geometry is non-colliding. Tests cover the catalog, actual achievement predicates, locked drafts, storage validation and independence, all parts across all seven families and both directions, and the shared particle and flight renderers. SVG DOM doubles do not verify the visual appearance of actual browsers. Browser/device QA remains outstanding, including short windows, real reloads, leftward symbols, preview animation cleanup and rapid fire.
