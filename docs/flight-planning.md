# PR9 — Flight planning dock

The temporary cannon dropdown is replaced by a compact horizontal rail of cannon cards with the user's own equation, family icon and a labelled state. All cards are visible where space permits; larger sets scroll inside the rail, not the document. One shared equation editor keeps the stage large, particularly on school desktops and narrow screens. The current card and corresponding cannon have matching selection highlights. Clicking/tapping a cannon or using Enter/Space on a focused cannon selects its editor while retaining coordinate inspection; hover only inspects. During flight and celebrations the dock and editor are locked, while coordinate inspection and camera controls remain available.

Readiness means syntax and supported function family only. Empty/invalid entries disable launch without losing a Pip. No equation is tested against destinations during planning. The immutable plan is captured on launch. On completed attempts, a reached leg that captures another cannon or the exit is labelled Tested; the final failed leg is Revise (including a loop). Unreached equations are not newly tested. Evidence is local to an unchanged equation and not a certificate of the entire route: editing any equation clears that cannon's evidence, not other independently tested legs. Even equivalent retyped expressions clear their evidence. A later attempt that fails before reaching a previously tested cannon can retain that local evidence. The latest failed cannon is selected automatically without erasing its trajectory. Editing clears the prior displayed trace, but keeps other cards' evidence. Restart clears all drafts; terminal replay retains equations but clears all test evidence. Level switches reset the unsaved plan. No solution preview, persistent progress, or advanced function editor yet.

Seven pure tests cover readiness, downstream misfires, evidence invalidation, stale results, unvisited cannons, loop failures and replay. Browser QA pending: select cards and scene cannons via mouse/touch/keyboard; check selection persists on resize/pan/zoom, no duplicate inputs and no prelaunch route; enter A=x, B=x+1 on relay and observe Tested/Revise; edit B to 8-3x/5 and verify A stays Tested, B Ready, then both Tested after success. Confirm switching cannons does not erase text or flight feedback, locked controls cannot edit, restart/replay behave correctly, and long expressions/multiple cards fit narrow and short screens.
