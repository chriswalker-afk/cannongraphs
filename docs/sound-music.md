# PR27 — Sound and music

Original, lightweight Web Audio synthesis, with a master mute, separate music/effects volume sliders, and a sound settings dialog from Home, gameplay, selection screens, the Flight Log and both workshops. Fresh installs are silent (master muted); the user's preferences are saved locally, separately from scores and cosmetics. A previously enabled setting still needs a trusted click/key gesture before creating/resuming an audio context. No third-party assets, network requests, autoplay at page load or account storage. Unsupported or blocked audio remains a silent, fully playable game. Settings storage failure keeps the session's settings.

Seven original looping themes (menu plus one per world) use short melodic and bass patterns with restrained volume. All menus/workshops share the menu theme; gameplay uses its world theme. Sound effects include loading, firing, capture/exit, misfire, splat, lost flight, gems, completion, achievements and game over. They use the existing deterministic attempt/rapid-fire timelines, not SVG redraws. Misfire never plays a firing/splat effect. Gem pickup sounds refer to provisional contacts, not permanent rewards; progress and achievements still require completed success. A single achievement or completion fanfare plays at terminal success. Home's decorative animation is silent except for optional menu music; the Cannon Workshop's explicit Test fire button can preview the fire sound.

The scheduler bounds polyphony and drops stale animation cues instead of playing catch-up bursts. Muting, hiding the page and navigating cancel scheduled voices; music stops while hidden and resumes only when appropriate. Reduced motion retains the same semantic sounds without adding animation. Audio is optional, never a mathematical hint or sole feedback channel, and does not change the simulation, budgets, scoring or access. Volume/mute changes act immediately without resetting the level. Browser QA remains necessary for actual sound quality, autoplay restrictions, device outputs, dialog focus, tab visibility, simultaneous effects and local persistence.
