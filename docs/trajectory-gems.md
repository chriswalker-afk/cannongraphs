# PR17 — Optional trajectory gems

Fourteen selected levels across the existing four worlds now contain optional turquoise gems. They have authored coordinates, visible inspectable diamond art and a fixed circular pickup radius of 0.14 world units. A Pip's nominal radius is 0.20; pickup occurs at continuous centre distance <= 0.34, with the existing geometry tolerance. The actual attempted line/parabola is restricted to its resolved leg before contacts are evaluated. The existing piecewise distance-extrema solver is used against a point, not sampled animation frames. Contacts are ordered by leg and progress; a gem can be collected once, even if multiple legs pass it. Misfires, untraversed legs and solid-contact ties cannot collect. Gems do not obstruct flight.

During the first Pip's flight, pickups are shown at their deterministic timeline positions with a brief sparkle and a small readout. A failed attempt restores every gem; nothing is banked until the Pip reaches the exit. A successful route banks only its collected unique gems, including a last-Pip success. The rapid-fire batch replays that route without multiplying rewards. The completion panel reports gems separately; missing any gem never blocks completion or changes the existing creature-based star rating. Restart/replay clears the run; future persistent progress/achievements can store the separate count. A gem may be inspected like other objects; its coordinate is visible without revealing a graph or reference equation.

Schema checks reject duplicate IDs, overlapping gems, gems intersecting terrain, anchor-adjacent gems and gems outside the world. All authored placements are audited with their reference routes in tests. Additional regressions cover tangencies, curved versus chord contact, reverse traversal, duplicate hits, misfires, blocked/unreached gems, timeline and scoring isolation.

This first pass adds gems selectively rather than cluttering every introductory level. Later worlds can author their own optional placements. Existing levels with no gems retain their original interface. Manual browser QA pending: inspect, collect, miss, retry and replay gems on desktop and touch, resize/zoom midflight, check reduced motion and long routes.
