# PR11 — Mathematical feedback

A completed failed attempt produces a short, cannon-specific mathematical prompt. The optional Details disclosure shows observed mathematical evidence from the submitted equation and flight result. It is not a prelaunch solver. The hint is explicitly labelled Last attempt and remains available while equations are edited; the old trajectory is cleared as before. Starting another attempt hides the previous prompt; success, restart, replay and level changes clear it. Neither hints nor editing change the Pip budget.

Failure categories: source mismatch (substitute the cannon coordinates; later rearrange y = mx + c), obstacle contact (actual bounds, surface normal and body clearance), missed passed destination (substitute its x-coordinate and compare y-values), boundary exit (check changes in x/y and slope), no forward destination (revisit earlier routing), and loops (observed cannon sequence). When several marked points are possible, a passed point is explicitly an example, chosen by smallest observed vertical miss; it is not declared the intended destination. Obstacle contact does not imply a unique slope correction. No correct coefficient, reference equation or unattempted trajectory is calculated or disclosed. Very small misses are displayed with significant figures rather than rounded to zero.

Support increases from a question to a substitution/check and then a general mathematical method. Counts are scoped to the current run, failed cannon and failure category (specific obstacle for collisions). Only unique completed attempt IDs increment counts. A different cannon or kind starts its own count; three is the maximum support level. The last failed cannon remains selected by PR9. Details can be opened at any stage and contain the actual flight evidence.

Nine new pure tests cover success, source mismatch, small errors, all terrain types, boundary and passed-point diagnosis, reverse directions, loops, escalation/idempotence and snapshot behavior. Manual browser QA remains: check first/second/third failure prompts, keyboard access to Details, preserving the prompt while adjusting a draft, hiding hints during flight, retry/restart and short/narrow viewports.
